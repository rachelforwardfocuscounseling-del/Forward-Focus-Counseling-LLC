Using out-of-network benefits

How superbills and insurance reimbursement work

Forward Focus Counseling is a private-pay practice and does not bill insurance directly. If your plan includes out-of-network mental health benefits, you may be eligible for reimbursement after paying for your session.

1. Pay for your session

The session fee is $205 for both in-person and virtual counseling.

2. Receive a superbill

A superbill can be provided with the information your insurance company may require when you request out-of-network reimbursement.

3. Submit it to your insurer

You submit the superbill to your insurance company. Your insurer may reimburse a portion of the session cost depending on your plan, deductible, and out-of-network benefits.

Questions to ask your insurance company

Before beginning therapy, you may want to ask:

  • Do I have out-of-network benefits for outpatient mental health services?
  • What is my out-of-network deductible, and how much of it have I met?
  • What percentage or amount does my plan reimburse for out-of-network therapy?
  • How do I submit a superbill for reimbursement?

Reimbursement is determined by your insurance plan and is not guaranteed. Please verify your benefits directly with your insurer.
