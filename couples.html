Couples therapy

Understand the cycle, not just the argument.

Emotionally focused couples therapy

Couples often become caught in recurring patterns where both partners are trying to protect the relationship or themselves, yet each response makes the other person's reaction more likely.

Using an EFT-informed approach, therapy helps slow those cycles down, identify the emotions and attachment needs underneath them, and create new experiences of being heard, understood, and responded to.

Common areas of focus

For clients in relationships, therapy may include exploration of relational dynamics, boundaries, communication patterns, emotional needs, recurring conflict, emotional distance, major decisions, transitions, trust, division of responsibilities, intimacy, and feeling unseen or unsupported.
