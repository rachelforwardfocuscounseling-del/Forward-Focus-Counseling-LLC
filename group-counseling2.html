Group Counseling in Alexandria, VA

A place to feel understood, connect with others, and move forward together.

At Forward Focus Counseling, small, therapist-led groups are offered in person and virtually, providing a warm, respectful setting to share experiences, gain perspective, and build practical tools for everyday life. For parents and caregivers

Parent Connection & Support Group

Supporting your child matters. So does supporting yourself.

Parenting a child with ADHD, autism spectrum differences, developmental needs, or ongoing behavioral and emotional challenges can bring both meaningful moments and real stress. Even when you are doing your best, you may feel overwhelmed, isolated, or unsure of what to try next.

This supportive counseling group is a place to connect with parents who understand the complexity of your experience. Together, we make room for honest conversation without judgment, listen to one another, and explore ways to respond to challenges with greater understanding, confidence, and compassion.

What we may explore together

  • Navigating challenging behaviors and understanding the needs beneath them
  • Supporting children with ADHD, autism, and differing developmental or sensory needs
  • Managing parental stress, frustration, worry, and emotional exhaustion
  • Strengthening communication, connection, and routines at home
  • Developing practical strategies while caring for your own mental health

The goal is not perfect parenting. It is helping parents feel less alone, more supported, and better equipped to create a calmer, more connected home life.

Ages 22–26

Young Adult Connection & Support Group

Finding your people and feeling more comfortable being yourself.

Building friendships and meaningful relationships in young adulthood is not always easy. If you experience loneliness, social anxiety, low mood, or difficulty connecting with others, you are not alone.

This small, therapist-led group offers a welcoming space to talk about what feels hard, practice connecting with others, and explore how to build confidence in social situations. Participants can share experiences, learn from one another, and take manageable steps toward the relationships and community they want.

What we may explore together

  • Loneliness, isolation, and the desire for belonging
  • Social anxiety and self-consciousness in relationships
  • Starting and maintaining friendships
  • Communication, vulnerability, and healthy boundaries
  • Building confidence, connection, and emotional well-being

Format: 60-minute sessions, offered in person or virtually. Please contact us for current group scheduling and availability.
